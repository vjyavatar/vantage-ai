A realistic day

Northwind runs a support bot. Last month the bot answered 8,000 tickets and the model invoice was $4,200.

  1. The bot stays in the help desk. Nobody rebuilds it.
  2. The engineer changes one address, from the model vendor to https://vantage-ai-3dti.onrender.com/v1.
  3. Support gets its own key, like a separate expense card. Sales can have another.
  4. A ticket comes in. Vantage prices a lower-cost model and a stronger model, then keeps the running average.
  5. If that average is below the old sticker price, the saving is locked and the reply goes back to the customer.
  6. Finance opens this page and sees direct shop, Vantage cost, and the gap for that team.
