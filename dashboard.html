Think of it like this

Grocery run

You need milk. One store charges $5. Another charges $3. Vantage is the person who checks both and only buys when the basket is cheaper. Your recipe does not change.

Taxi vs shared ride

The destination is the same. The meter is not. A direct model is the private taxi at the airport rate. Vantage is the dispatcher who sends the ride that still arrives, at a lower fare.

Wholesale buyer

A shop sells a shirt for $40. The buyer sources fabric and stitching for $26 and only locks the order if the total stays under $40. That locked gap is the saving.
